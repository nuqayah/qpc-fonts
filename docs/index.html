Other fonts general Arabic text · not Qur'an-specific

Uthman Naskh is a general-purpose Arabic text face from the KFGQPC — not a Qur'an font and not tied to any riwaya. Sample text is a saying of Wuhayb ibn al-Ward on the Qur'an.

Uthman Naskhother-fonts/fonts/UthmanTN1 Ver10.*
قال وهيب بن الورد رحمه الله: لم نجد شيئًا أرقَّ لهذه القلوب ولا أشد استجلابًا للحق من قراءة القرآن لمن تدبَّره.

Icon-font glyph inventories QPC Symbols & QCF_BSML

These fonts are glyph inventories — each codepoint draws a single figure (a mark or a ligature), not a letter you can compose. Other QPC fonts (text and page-glyph) are demonstrated above in the riwaya sections.
